# Body Reset Coach

A personal health and fitness coach in one web page, by Fahim Muntasir Sami.

Open it on your phone: **https://fmsami.github.io/health-coach/**

The first time you open it, it asks for your age, height, weight, activity and wake-up time, and works out everything else from those:

- **Today**: a timed daily checklist of 13 tasks built around your wake-up time (tick each one when you've done it), a reminder card showing what's due now and next, water and step counters, and a morning weigh-in.
- **Workouts**: a 12-week, low-impact home program in three phases (two strength sessions, walking days, a long walk and a rest day each week), with easier and harder versions of every exercise.
- **Food**: calorie and protein targets (Mifflin-St Jeor, 500 kcal deficit), the plate method, a sample day of South Asian meals and a table of easy swaps.
- **Progress**: weight chart with a 7-day average and the planned path, milestones with estimated dates, an 8-week habit heatmap and a weigh-in log.
- **Doctor's list**: blood tests to ask for, a STOP-BANG sleep apnea screen, warning signs, and when to see a doctor first.
- **My details**: your details, plus backup and restore.

## Install on iPhone

1. Open **https://fmsami.github.io/health-coach/** in **Safari** (not Chrome).
2. Tap the **Share** button, then **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.
3. Open **Body Reset** from your home screen. It runs full-screen and works offline.

Your data is saved on your phone, inside the app. Use **My details → Copy backup** now and then and keep the text somewhere safe.

## Reminders

- **While the app is open**: press **Turn on reminders** on the Today tab. At each task's time a pop-up appears with a chime, with **Done, tick it** and **Remind me in 15 min** buttons.
- **When the app is closed**: press **Add reminders to my phone calendar** on the Today tab and add the file to your calendar. It adds a daily alarm for every task, plus your workout for each day of the week. `reminders.ics` in this repo is the same file for a 7:00 am wake-up.

This is general guidance, not medical advice. See a doctor before starting if you have heart, blood pressure, sugar or joint problems.

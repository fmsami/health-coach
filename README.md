# Body Reset Coach

A personal health and fitness coach app for iPhone, by Fahim Muntasir Sami.

Open it on your phone: **https://fmsami.github.io/health-coach/**

The first time you open it, it asks for your age, height, weight, activity and wake-up time, and works out everything else from those.

## What's inside

**Today**
- Timed daily checklist of 13 tasks built around your wake-up time, plus your own tasks. Tick each one when you've done it.
- Reminder card showing what's due now and next, with pop-up reminders and a chime while the app is open.
- Streak counter, coach tip of the day, Craving SOS (10-minute timer and steps) and 2-minute calm breathing.
- Water and step counters, daily check-in (sleep, mood, energy) and morning weigh-in with waist.

**Coach**
- A chat coach that sees your checklist, food, workouts, weight, sleep, readings and notes. Ask what you missed, what to eat instead of vegetables, or how to recover from a bad day.
- Remembers lasting facts about you ("I don't eat vegetables", "bad left knee") in an editable notes list.
- In the private Claude link it uses your Claude account. In the installed app it uses your own Anthropic API key, which stays only on your phone (never in the code, the repo or backups).

**Train**
- Guided workouts: tap each set as you finish it, and a rest timer starts on its own.
- A "Watch how it's done" YouTube link on every exercise and stretch.
- A 12-week low-impact home program in three phases, with easier and harder versions of every exercise.
- Walk and cardio log, workout history and weekly totals.

**Food**
- Food diary with 60+ Bangladeshi and everyday foods, your own saved foods, and calories and protein against your target.
- 7-day meal plan (about 1,800–1,950 kcal a day) with one-tap logging and a button that adds the week's food to your shopping list.
- Guide: targets (Mifflin-St Jeor, 500 kcal deficit), the plate method, a sample day and easy swaps.
- Six simple healthy deshi recipes and a shopping list.

**Progress**
- Weight chart with a 7-day average and the planned path.
- Weekly report with "Coach says" advice, 19 badges, milestones with estimated dates, an 8-week habit heatmap and a weigh-in log.
- Body: waist, neck, hips, chest, arm and thigh measurements with a body fat % estimate (US Navy tape method), fat and lean mass, and history.
- Photos: weekly front, side and back progress photos with a before-and-now comparison. Photos stay on the phone and are never uploaded.

**Health**
- Blood pressure and blood sugar log (mmol/L or mg/dL) with standard ranges.
- Checkups to ask your doctor for, a STOP-BANG sleep apnea screen, and warning signs.
- Sleep and stress help.

**Me**
- Your details, your own checklist tasks, reminders, phone calendar alarms, install steps, and backup and restore.

## Install on iPhone

1. Open **https://fmsami.github.io/health-coach/** in **Safari** (not Chrome).
2. Tap the **Share** button, then **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.
3. Open **Body Reset** from your home screen. It runs full-screen and works offline.

Your data is saved on your phone, inside the app. Use **Me → App & backup → Copy backup** every week or two and keep the text in Notes.

## Reminders

- **While the app is open**: press **Turn on reminders**. At each task's time a pop-up appears with a chime, with **Done, tick it** and **Remind me in 15 min** buttons.
- **When the app is closed**: press **Add reminders to my phone calendar** and add the file to your calendar. It adds a daily alarm for every task, plus your workout for each day of the week. `reminders.ics` in this repo is the same file for a 7:00 am wake-up.

This is general guidance, not medical advice. See a doctor before starting if you have heart, blood pressure, sugar or joint problems.
